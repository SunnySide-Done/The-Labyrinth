                  The Labyrinth (tutorial)

You land on soft, warm soil that cushions your fall. You look up at the charging point, but it is nowhere to be seen. You stare up at stalactites.

You turn your attention back to the cave. Moist warm air surrounds you, water dripping off the moss creeping along the stones. There are brown feathers littered across the floor.

Three paths lay before you, but they are too dark to see into.

You look at your hands – a red thread is tied around your ring finger.

A piercing shriek reverberates from the left tunnel.

Before you can run, a harpy charges towards you, flapping her large, bird-like wings towards you, blocking your path.

You are trapped.
